Policy comparison

One scenario, four policies, identical seeds.

  1. Static + distance: fixed prices and shortest driving paths. This is the baseline.
  2. Dynamic + distance: change prices using occupancy and demand forecasts.
  3. Static + time: fixed prices and paths using simulated traffic delays.
  4. Dynamic + time: combine pricing changes with travel-time routing.

Run a comparison to see recorded outcomes and uncertainty. All results are simulations; demand and behavior have not been calibrated to field observations.

Each estimate includes a 95% confidence interval across seeds. A paired difference whose interval contains zero is inconclusive. Driver outcomes describe arrivals after the warm-up; occupancy and revenue exclude the warm-up. Intervals describe simulation sampling; they do not account for uncertainty in the demand or behavior model. No single metric defines the best policy.

Search, drive, walk, and stay-cost averages describe successful drivers only. Policies may serve different driver populations. Driving time is recorded separately from simulated search delay. Walking distance is a straight-line estimate; accessibility of the pedestrian route is unverified.
